Hi again! Its movie time!

Having books translated into movies is always a monumental task, having to shorten a lot of things and change the story to have it all fit into the final time stamp, and even with the changes many scenes still get cut out due to lack of time.
So let me show-- and tell you some of my favorite scenes that dident make the final cut!

My favorite character in the francise is a character named 'Frypan', The Glade's cook. In the book there are a few short discriptions of him at work and mentions of him and his cooking. But I was sad to see in the movie, its practicly impossible to even tell he was a cook if it wasent for the direct calling out of his role, nor were there any examples of him taking on the big role of feeding, The Glade of 50 boys. And I was even sadder to find out there was ONE scene that did show him in a makeshift kitchen, dishing out food. That of course-- was removed.
